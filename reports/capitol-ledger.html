Leaderboard

Read before you trust any of it

  • None of this is evidence of coordination, insider knowledge, or wrongdoing. Every method here measures correlation; the chance-correction and baseline steps make surviving correlations harder to explain by luck alone — they don't explain why one exists.
  • Coverage is partial. Only 42.5% of Trump's disclosed trades (3,294 of 7,744) resolve to a clean ticker — the rest is bonds, money-market funds, and small-caps with no clean symbol. Committee assignments matched 200 of 355 members.
  • Both sides can disclose late. STOCK Act gives Congress 45 days to report a trade; Trump's OGE filings were sometimes over a year late. A close match on trade date doesn't mean either side could have known about the other's trade in real time.
  • Full methodology, caveats, and the underlying scripts live in analysis/RESULTS.md in the repo. This page is a static snapshot of that analysis, not a live feed.
